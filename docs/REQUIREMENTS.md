## Quality
Test mobile widths including 320, 390 and 430px, tablet and desktop; Android Chrome, iOS Safari and WhatsApp in-app browsers. Target initial assets under 1 MB and load under 3 seconds on good 4G, subject to measurement. Accessible controls, reduced motion, error/loading states, secure server validation and no location tracking.

## Out of scope
