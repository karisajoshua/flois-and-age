# Design system

## Direction
Luxury ivory and champagne-gold wedding stationery. Mobile-first, cinematic, chapter-based experience rather than a conventional website.

## Proposed tokens
Ivory #FFF9EF; paper #F4EBDD; champagne #E8D5B7; beige #D5BF9F; antique gold #AD8B50; deep gold #8C6935; dark brown #403429. Use dark brown for readable body text; verify contrast.

Typography: Great Vibes (calligraphy), Cormorant Garamond (headings), Lora (body), Inter (admin). Verify Å glyph and font licenses.

## Assets
Separate envelope body, flap, interior, wax seal F & Å, invitation card, silk/paper textures, floral corners, petals, rings, ornaments, storybook cover/pages, RSVP and thank-you stationery. Approved couple and venue photos. Licensed optional instrumental. Prefer SVG or transparent WebP/PNG for layers; AVIF/WebP for photos.

## Motion
GSAP for envelope and cinematic timeline; Motion for gestures and transitions. Use transforms and opacity, avoid blocking content. Provide skip, reduced motion, audio opt-in, and direct RSVP/directions access. Do not force swipe-only navigation.

## Layout
Full-screen chapters with safe-area padding and accessible touch targets. Responsive desktop fallback. Dashboard uses clear functional layouts rather than decorative animation.
